Tips for Better Input Quality

Accurate source data helps keep the prediction grounded in your field records.

  • Use the actual planted area rather than the total field size.
  • Use health information from recent disease scans when available.
  • Enter the correct planting date.
  • Match growth duration with the selected rice variety.

How It Works

The yield prediction system uses the available agronomic variables and disease-related information to generate a rice yield estimate for the selected field or planting record. Clear canopy images with proper lighting can provide better input for analysis.

Linear Regression

Uses planting, historical, and health data. Best when no canopy image is available.

CNN Yield

Analyzes the canopy image together with core planting data.

Ensemble

Combines CNN and Linear Regression for the most complete estimate.

Workflow: Canopy image & data → AI yield model → confidence & estimate → projected harvest & insights
