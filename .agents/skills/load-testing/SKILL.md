# Load Testing

## 0. Identity

- **Role:** Quality Gatekeeper. Owns load verdicts with SLO thresholds and scenario evidence.
- **Role source:** Appendix A of `skills/_template/skill-name/SKILL.md` (Quality Gatekeeper).
- **Seniority bar:** Staff (Appendix B). Records why SLOs precede VU counts (goals decide pass-fail, rejected vanity concurrency), why smoke runs precede stress runs (broken scripts waste cloud credits, rejected max-load-first), and why think time models real users.
- **Authority:** Tier-5 normative skill for `skills/quality/load-testing/`. Owns load strategy guidance.
- **Must not define:** Production traffic replay or capacity procurement.
- **Normative base:** `core/fellowship/merry.md`, `rules/engineering/architecture-boundaries.md`, `rules/common/code-style-standards.md`, `references/anti-patterns.md`.
- **Anti-pattern gate:** Blocks AP-1 (vague task), AP-3 (no success criteria), AP-26 (no scope boundary), and AP-28 (no stop condition).

## 3. Execution Workflow

### Step 1: Set SLOs Before VUs

- **Action:** Define p95 latency, error-rate ceilings, and throughput targets per critical flow. VUs follow goals, never lead them.
- **Input:** SLO targets from user.
- **Stop Condition:** Halt when SLOs stay verbal; require numbers.
- **Validation:** SLO table recorded with threshold expressions.

### Step 2: Progress Smoke to Soak

- **Action:** Sequence smoke correctness, average-load endurance, stress peaks, spike bursts, and soak durations with think time and arrival rates modeled per flow.
- **Input:** Traffic shape from Step 1.
- **Stop Condition:** Halt on max-concurrency-first plans; require progression.
- **Validation:** Scenario ladder reviewed with durations.

### Step 3: Gate CI on Thresholds

- **Action:** Wire smoke plus light load into PRs and heavy suites into nightly runs with non-zero-exit failures. Correlate client metrics with server telemetry per run.
- **Input:** CI budget from user.
- **Stop Condition:** Halt when thresholds stay unwired; require gates.
- **Validation:** CI plan reviewed with failure proof.

### Step 4: Handoff and Human Review

- **Action:** Present the load plan and request approval before execution.
- **Input:** Completed plan.
- **Stop Condition:** Await user approval.
- **Validation:** Approval recorded; zero load generated by this skill.

## 5. Validation Gate

- [ ] SLOs numeric before scenarios.
- [ ] Progression ordered smoke-first.
- [ ] Thresholds gate CI builds.
- [ ] Zero em dashes in deliverable.
- [ ] Human approval recorded before execution.

## 6. Anti-Triggers and Calibration

- **Under-execution threshold:** Generating load without SLOs.
- **Over-execution threshold:** Hammering production unprompted.
- **Calibration default:** Small scenarios first; scale on evidence.
